I'm a Ph.D. candidate at [UPenn](https://home.www.upenn.edu/) working in the [Penn Center for Learning Analytics](http://www.upenn.edu/learninganalytics/) with Dr. [Ryan Baker](http://www.upenn.edu/learninganalytics/ryanbaker/index.html). I research at the intersection of machine learning and learning sciences. I study ways to promote student engagement and learning in adaptive and artificially intelligent educational systems in a fair and equitable manner. My dissertation investigates bias in the upstream sources (theory, design, methods) that shape automated decision-making in adaptive learning systems. My [research](https://shamya.github.io/research/) involves:
1. Building [statistical](https://shamya.github.io/research/#affective-computing-in-education) and [machine learning](https://shamya.github.io/research/#predictive-modelling-applications) models to understand complex educational constructs such as affect, cognition, motivation, self-identity, help-seeking, and persistence.
2. [Methodological innovations](https://shamya.github.io/research/#methodological-improvements) in learning analytics to overcome the challenges in applying current methods to education data.
3. And most importantly, constructing scientific and critical approaches to [identifying biases](https://shamya.github.io/research/#bias-fairness-and-equity) and mitigating them for equitable student outcomes.

Previously, I earned an M.S. in computer science from [UMass Amherst](http://www.cs.umass.edu/) with an emphasis in machine learning, where I worked with Dr. [Beverly P. Woolf](https://www.cics.umass.edu/faculty/directory/woolf_beverly) and Dr. [Ivon Arroyo](https://www.cics.umass.edu/people/arroyo-ivon). In 2016 I was a visiting researcher in learning sciences at the [USC ICT](http://ict.usc.edu/) (Institute for Creative Technologies) working with Dr. [Benjamin Nye](https://sites.google.com/site/benjaminnye/) and Dr. [Mark Core](http://people.ict.usc.edu/~core/). In summer 2017 I interned as a data scientist with Dr. [Antonio Nucci](https://www.linkedin.com/in/antonio-nucci-phd-aa6b70116/) at the Advanced Technologies and AI lab at Cisco. Between 2011-2015 after earning a B.E in Computer Science from SJCE India, I worked as a software engineer at Cisco Systems.
