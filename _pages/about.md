I'm a Ph.D. candidate at [UPenn](https://home.www.upenn.edu/) working in the [Penn Center for Learning Analytics](http://www.upenn.edu/learninganalytics/) with Dr. [Ryan Baker](http://www.upenn.edu/learninganalytics/ryanbaker/index.html). I research at the intersection of machine learning and learning sciences. I study ways to promote student engagement and learning in adaptive and artificially intelligent educational systems in a fair and equitable manner. My dissertation investigates bias in the upstream sources (theory, design, methods) that shape automated decision-making in adaptive learning systems. My [research](https://shamya.github.io/research/) involves:
1. Building models of complex educational constructs such as affect, cognition, motivation, self-identity, help-seeking, and persistence.
2. Methodological innovations in statistics and machine learning to overcome the challenges in applying current methods to education data.
3. And most importantly, constructing scientific and critical approaches to identifying and mitigating biases for equitable student outcomes.

Previously, I earned an M.S. in Computer Science from [UMass Amherst](http://www.cs.umass.edu/) with an emphasis in machine learning, where I worked with Dr. [Beverly P. Woolf](https://www.cics.umass.edu/faculty/directory/woolf_beverly) and Dr. [Ivon Arroyo](https://www.cics.umass.edu/people/arroyo-ivon). In 2016 I was a visiting researcher in learning sciences at the [USC Institute for Creative Technologies](http://ict.usc.edu/) working with Dr. [Benjamin Nye](https://sites.google.com/site/benjaminnye/) and Dr. [Mark Core](http://people.ict.usc.edu/~core/). In summer 2017 I interned as a Data Scientist with Dr. [Antonio Nucci](https://www.linkedin.com/in/antonio-nucci-phd-aa6b70116/) at the Advanced Technologies and AI lab at Cisco. Between 2011-2015 after earning a B.E in Computer Science from SJCE India, I worked as a Software Engineer at [Cisco Systems](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/index.html).
