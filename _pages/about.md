I am a postdoctoral fellow at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/) in the [Human-Computer Interaction Institute](https://www.hcii.cmu.edu/) working with Dr. [Vincent Aleven](http://www.cs.cmu.edu/~aleven/) and Dr. [Nikol Rummel](https://ife.rub.de/institut/personen/node/230). I earned my PhD from the [University of Pennsylvania](https://home.www.upenn.edu/) in 2021 working as a research fellow in the [Penn Center for Learning Analytics](http://www.upenn.edu/learninganalytics/) with Dr. [Ryan Baker](http://www.upenn.edu/learninganalytics/ryanbaker/index.html). I will join the [Learning Sciences](https://edpsych.education.wisc.edu/academics/learning-sciences/research/) faculty at the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://www.wisc.edu/) as an assistant professor in Jan 2023.

I study ways to promote student engagement and learning in adaptive and artificially intelligent educational systems in a fair and equitable manner. [My research](https://shamya.github.io/research/) is at the intersection of machine learning and learning sciences. My work often involves:
1. Building [statistical](https://shamya.github.io/research/#affective-computing-in-education) and [machine learning](https://shamya.github.io/research/#predictive-modelling-applications) models to understand complex educational constructs such as affect, cognition, motivation, self-identity, help-seeking, and persistence.
2. [Methodological innovations](https://shamya.github.io/research/#methodological-improvements) in learning analytics to overcome the challenges in applying current methods to education data.
3. And more importantly, constructing scientific and critical approaches to [identifying biases](https://shamya.github.io/research/#bias-fairness-and-equity) and mitigating them for equitable student outcomes.

Previously, I earned an MS in computer science from [UMass Amherst](http://www.cs.umass.edu/) with an emphasis in machine learning, where I worked with Dr. [Beverly P. Woolf](https://www.cics.umass.edu/faculty/directory/woolf_beverly) and Dr. [Ivon Arroyo](https://www.cics.umass.edu/people/arroyo-ivon). In 2016 I was a visiting researcher in learning sciences at [USC ICT](http://ict.usc.edu/) (Institute for Creative Technologies) working with Dr. [Benjamin Nye](https://sites.google.com/site/benjaminnye/) and Dr. [Mark Core](http://people.ict.usc.edu/~core/). In summer 2017 I interned as a data scientist with Dr. [Antonio Nucci](https://www.linkedin.com/in/antonio-nucci-phd-aa6b70116/) at the Advanced Technologies and AI lab at Cisco. Between 2011-2015 after earning a BE in Computer Science from SJCE India, I worked as a software engineer at Cisco.
