From 2017-2020, I designed and taught three iterations of [EXACT](https://www.gse.upenn.edu/innovation/exact)(Experiences in Applied Computational Thinking) - the certificate program offered by [Penn GSE](https://www.gse.upenn.edu/)(Graduate School of Education) in collaboration with Penn Engineering’s [GRASP lab](https://www.grasp.upenn.edu/). 

EXACT is a 30-week online, synchronous professional development program designed for K-12 educators to integrate their current pedagogical practices with computational thinking pertaining to the use of modern computational tools and practices in the context of their subject matter.

With [Catalyst @ Penn GSE](https://www.gse.upenn.edu/catalyst) in 2019, I won two [PASmart advancing grants](https://www.education.pa.gov/Policy-Funding/SchoolGrants/PAsmart/Pages/AdvancingGrants.aspx) of around $500K each from the Pennsylvania Department of Education to implement it in our school districts: 1) Computational Thinking in Action (CTIA) and 2) Eastern Pennsylvania Innovation Catalyst (EPIC) Network.
